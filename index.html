Research

I study how machines infer physical dynamics from what they see. Instead of training end-to-end predictors, I decompose visual representations along the causal structure of the physical process, so that geometry, material and dynamics can each be checked and adapted. My recent work, DELPHI and ADYTON establish a way to learn 4D deformable dynamics from RGB-D videos. We aim to build a general 4D Physics World Model for robotic decision-making.
